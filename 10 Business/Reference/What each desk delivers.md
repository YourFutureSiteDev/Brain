# What each desk delivers

Written 16 August 2026, taking the shape from
[[The AI content team, seven employees on Claude Code]] and applying it to the
desks we actually have. Every desk states four things and is held to them:

* **Delivers.** What lands on Byron's desk when it has finished. Not what it is
  working on, and not which number it moved.
* **Steps.** Three to five, in order, always the same ones.
* **Ratio.** Its own attrition, in figures. The content team's hook writer says
  10 written, 3 survive, 1 posts. A role that publishes its own kill rate can be
  held to it.
* **Now.** One phrase for what it is doing this minute.

The figures below are real, read from the database on 16 August 2026, and are
there to be looked up again rather than believed.

## CMO, from the Researcher

* **Delivers** the bot's search time spent on areas and niches that are not
  already saturated.
* **Steps** discover, collect, report.
* **Ratio** 238 found, 308 qualified, 243 skipped in one sweep. A sweep is 5220
  searches across 60 live areas of 403 and 4 niches.
* **Now** "sweeping 60 areas, 4 niches".

## CRO, from the Analyst

* **Delivers** leads actually moving toward a paid job, which here means getting
  a human on the phone.
* **Steps** track, analyse, learn, optimise.
* **Ratio** 80 emailed, 0 replied, 50 waiting in the call queue, 0 won. That is
  the number that matters most in the business and it is currently a zero.
* **Now** "50 waiting to be called".

## CDO, from the Analyst, the data half

* **Delivers** a straight answer on whether the lead data is worth acting on.
* **Steps** track, analyse, learn, optimise.
* **Ratio** 17129 leads, 4% with an email, 25% with a phone number, 358 held out
  of sending as UNKNOWN.
* **Now** "email coverage 4%".

## COO, from the Manager

* **Delivers** the bot running, at a safe volume, without jamming.
* **Steps** plan, assign, automate, track, publish daily.
* **Ratio** cycles run against cycles that actually sent. Today: many run, none
  sent, because it is the weekend.
* **Now** "cycle every 30 min, cap 15".

## CCO, from the Publisher

* **Delivers** an account that stays alive and a business that stays legal.
* **Steps** plan, schedule, publish, confirm, report.
* **Ratio** 23 suppressed against 80 sent, of which 1 is a real bounce, 1.25%.
* **Now** "8/8 compliance, sending shut for the weekend".

## CFO, with no counterpart

The content team has no money role, because it is not selling anything. So the
CFO inherits the shape and none of the content.

* **Delivers** a price that turns a job into profit rather than revenue.
* **Steps** price, cost, break even, hours per job, reprice when scope grows.
* **Ratio** quotes against jobs won. Currently 0 against 0.
* **Now** "£500 remake, £1000 newbuild, break even at 1 job a month".

## CEO, from the Manager's other half

* **Delivers** one source of truth, and a decision about whose job a thing is.
* **Steps** read, route, say who got it and why, remember.
* **Ratio** notes picked up from the vault against notes that reached a desk
  with a lever for them. On 15 August: 1 picked up, 0 that a rule could act on.
* **Now** "no lever anywhere for prose".

## The three nobody owns

The content team has seven employees and **three of them are creative**: the
hook writer owns the first two seconds, the script writer owns every word, the
designer owns every post.

We have six desks and **none of them owns the message.** There is no keyword for
a subject line, an opener, the wording, the pitch or the mockup anywhere in
`comms.DESKS`. The bot writes every email and builds every mockup and no desk is
accountable for whether either is any good.

That is not a small gap. It is 80 emails and 0 replies with nobody whose job it
is to care.

Until it has a desk of its own, **the CRO owns it**, on the grounds that it
already owns the reply rate and the message is the thing that produces replies.
It should hold:

* **Delivers** an opener worth answering.
* **Steps** draft, cut, hand off. Ten openings, keep what stops a reader, the
  winner goes out.
* **Ratio** the hook writer's own: 10 written, 3 survive, 1 sends. We currently
  write one and send it.
* **Now** "one opener, never tested".
