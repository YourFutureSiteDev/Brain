I have kept a note in the vault, "The AI content team, seven employees on Claude
Code". It is someone else's setup, seven AI employees who make content, and the
idea I want from it is that each of them states two things about itself: what it
delivers, and the three to five steps it takes to get there.

Hold each desk to both. Not what it is busy with and not which number it moved,
but what lands on my desk when it has finished, and the steps in order. Where a
desk has no answer, tell me which one, because that is the one I want to look at
first.
