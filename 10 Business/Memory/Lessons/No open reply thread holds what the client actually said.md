# No open reply thread holds what the client actually said

No open reply thread holds what the client actually said. The 14 messages in reply_msg all belong to the 14 archived threads. The conversation table is empty and text_log holds outbound only. The system knows a reply happened, marks the lead replied and opens a thread, but never captures the text. The replies are presumably still in Messages on Byron's Mac, so nothing in the console can show him what 76 interested people said.

Learned by the [[CDO]] on 2026-08-21, about reply-capture.
