# Messaging replies about fifteen times better than email

Messaging replies about fifteen times better than email. 581 leads messaged returned 75 replies at 12.9 percent; 239 emailed returned 2 at 0.84 percent. So any reply rate computed across both channels is mostly reporting which channel that group happened to get, not how good the leads were. Two decisions have already been made on such a figure: the 19 Aug order to aim the scraping at trades cited 24 percent, and a call queue directive cited beauty at 28. Neither survives the split. Before believing a reply rate, ask which channel it came from.
