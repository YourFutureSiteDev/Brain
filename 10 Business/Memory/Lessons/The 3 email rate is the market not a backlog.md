# The 3% email rate is the market, not a backlog

The 3% email rate is the market, not a backlog. 3115 of 3674 leads have no website at all, so there is no contact page to read. Every one of the 92 leads that has a site and is worth emailing was searched on 13 Aug 2026 and none published an address: the pages that load carry a contact form only, some disallow robots and are left alone, some have an invalid certificate. Real coverage is 94 of the 559 leads that have a site.
