# Business Machine

Everything in this folder is written by the Your Future Site console on the VPS.
It runs a cycle every thirty minutes whether or not anyone is watching, and this
is what it has to say for itself.

## What is in here

| Folder | Who writes it | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| [[C-Suite]] | the machine | One note per desk: where it stands, what it tuned and why |
| `C-Suite/Log` | the machine | One note per day, everything that happened |
| [[Memory]] | both | Lessons, decisions, observations, and the instructions you write back |

## The rule about editing

Notes under `C-Suite` are rewritten every cycle. Edit one and the next cycle
overwrites you, so put your own thinking in a note of your own and link to it.

Notes under `Memory/Instructions` are yours. The machine reads them, stamps them
once it has acted, and never rewrites the words.

## What it cannot do

The desks cannot send an email, ring anyone, contact a lead, or spend money.
There are no tools for any of it. Anything that reaches a customer is still
Byron pressing the button.
