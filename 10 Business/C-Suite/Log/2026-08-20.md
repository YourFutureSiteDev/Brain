- **CRO** Read outside on when a small business owner cold called about a website says no, what is the actual objection and is there a solid rebuttal that fits a one person, no-ad-spend business: The most common no is not price, it is 'I already get enough work from word of mouth or my Facebook page, I don't need a website.' The specific counter that holds up: Facebook pages do not show up when someone searches Google for the trade plus suburb, and the business does not own that page, Meta controls who sees it and organic reach on it has been falling for years. A website is not a replacement for word of mouth or Facebook, it is what confirms the business is legitimate once someone has heard the name and gone looking. (source: Buildify NZ 'Do Tradies Need a Website in 2026' (buildifysites.co.nz) and Groundwork Digital 'Do Tradies Need a Website' (groundworkdigital.co.nz), both 2026)
