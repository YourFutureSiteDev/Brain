# Capture and organise are different jobs

Deciding where a thought goes, at the moment you have it, kills the thought.

## Why it matters

Capture has to cost nothing. Any friction, even a two second "which folder", trains you to stop capturing. That is how note systems die: not from being messy, but from going quiet.

So the inbox exists to be a dumping ground, and filing is a separate job done on a schedule. Two different modes, two different times, never the same keystroke.

## Evidence

Every abandoned notes app has a beautiful folder structure and forty notes in it.
