# Your Future Site speaks as a team, never as Byron

Everything customer-facing uses the business identity. The domain is `yourfuturesite.dev` and the address is `hello@yourfuturesite.dev`. The voice is "we" and "the Your Future Site team". Byron's name appears on no website, no email and no signature.

## Why it matters

Two reasons, both good. A one-person operation reading as a team is normal positioning and removes an objection before it forms. It also keeps a school-age founder's name off cold email to strangers.

## How to apply

Check for first-person singular before anything ships. In [[Local Outreach Bot]] this lives in the sender block of `config.json` and in the copy strings in `src/compose.py` and `src/company.py`.
