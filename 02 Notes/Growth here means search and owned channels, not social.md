# Growth here means search and owned channels, not social

Traffic comes from Google and from lists that are owned outright. No social media, decided and then re-confirmed when the option was offered again.

## Why it matters

It rules out a whole category of work permanently, which is the point. Every plan that routes through posting is off the table before it gets drafted, so effort concentrates on search, email and direct outreach.
