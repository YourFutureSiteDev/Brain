# Automate the boring half, keep the human half

The bot emails. Byron calls.

## Why it matters

Research and first contact are volume work, and volume work is where a machine wins outright. Conversation is where a person wins, and it is also the part that converts.

Automating the whole funnel would raise volume and lower the close rate to near zero. Automating none of it caps the pipeline at how much time is left after school.

The split is not a limitation. It is the design.
