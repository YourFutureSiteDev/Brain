# Page one Maps results are the wrong prospects

The businesses ranking first for a competitive search already have a good website. That is partly why they rank.

## Why it matters

It inverts how prospecting feels like it should work. The instinct is to go after the visible, successful businesses. Those are exactly the ones with nothing to buy.

The real leads sit deeper in the results, in quieter suburbs, and in less contested niches. Harder to find, far more likely to say yes.

## Evidence

Found while building the Maps research for [[Local Outreach Bot]]. The related discovery from the same work: driving Google Maps search through a browser yields name, rating, review count, phone and website host with no API key and no billing. Extracting full hrefs trips the browser tool's cookie and query-string blocker, so return hostnames only.
