# Doing the work is not the bottleneck, being found is

Four buildable businesses exist here. Sites, bots, demos and products, all real and all working. Revenue is still near zero, and none of the gap is a build problem.

## Why it matters

The pull is always toward building, because building is legible and finishable. Distribution is neither. So the sensible-feeling choice is another feature, and the sensible-feeling choice is wrong roughly every time.

## Evidence

[[Local Outreach Bot]] is complete and sending is switched off. [[Hybrid RP Storefront]] is designed and has no packages in it. In both cases the remaining step is small, unglamorous, and not code.
