# People

One note per person who matters: clients, prospects who replied, teachers, anyone whose context is worth holding.

Use the `Person` template. The parts that earn their keep are **Threads**, the open loops with that person, and **Log**, so the next conversation starts where the last one ended rather than from nothing.

Prospects who have not replied stay in the outreach pipeline, not here. This folder is for people, not leads.
