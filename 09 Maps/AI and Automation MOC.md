# AI and Automation MOC

Things that run without being asked.

## Running

- **[[Local Outreach Bot]]**
  Always on, cycles every thirty minutes.
- **[[Fine Line Schedule Watcher]]**
  Watches for new PDFs.

## Principles

- **[[Automate the boring half, keep the human half]]**
  Volume work to the machine, conversation to the person.
- **[[An always-on job needs a stop button in the app, not the terminal]]**
  You are never at a terminal when you want it stopped.

## macOS traps

- **[[LaunchAgents cannot run from the Desktop]]**
  Fails at login before a line runs.
- **[[Desktop files carry provenance that blocks writes]]**
  New files fine, existing files refused.
- **[[Always test IPv4 and IPv6 separately]]**
  One average hid the whole answer.
