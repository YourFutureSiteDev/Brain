# Archive

Finished and abandoned work. Out of the way, still fully searchable, still linked from wherever it was linked before.

Nothing is ever deleted from here. A dead project explains why it died, and that is often the most useful note in the vault.

When archiving, change the status tag to `#status/done` or `#status/dead` and add one line at the top saying what happened.
