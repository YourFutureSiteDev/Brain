# Naming conventions

- **Notes**: sentence case, no punctuation at the end. `Atomic notes make the graph useful`
- **Daily notes**: `YYYY-MM-DD`. Sorts correctly forever.
- **Projects**: the thing, not the verb. `Local Outreach Bot`, not `Build the outreach bot`.
- **People**: full name as you would say it. `Jane Cooper`.
- **Sources**: title first, then author if disambiguation is needed. `Building a Second Brain (Forte)`.
- **Attachments**: whatever they came as. They live in `99 Attachments` and are never linked by name.

Never put a date, a version, or a status in a file name. All three change, and renaming breaks nothing in Obsidian but breaks everything in your head.
