Page one Maps results are the wrong prospects

The businesses ranking first for a competitive search already have a good website. That is partly why they rank. It inverts how prospecting feels like it should work: the instinct is to go after the visible, successful businesses, and those are exactly the ones with nothing to buy.

The real leads sit deeper in the results, in quieter suburbs, and in less contested niches.
