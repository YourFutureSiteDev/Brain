# Writing

Newsletters, product copy, cold email, and the notes in this vault.

## Standard

Every piece has one idea and one reader. If you cannot name both, it is not ready.

## Where it goes out

- [[Money Road To 1K]], newsletter
- [[Local Outreach Bot]], cold email copy
- [[AI At Work]], marketing

## Rules

- No dashes as punctuation. Commas, periods and colons do the same jobs better.
- Business voice is "we", never "I". See [[Your Future Site speaks as a team, never as Byron]].
- Never state an unverifiable fact about a prospect. The bot enforces this and so should the human.

## Recurring

- Weekly: one permanent note turned into something publishable
